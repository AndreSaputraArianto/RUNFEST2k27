Twenty thousand runners. One city. One morning.

RUN FEST closes the city's main avenues and turns them into a start-to-finish celebration of effort. Elite athletes and first-timers share the same line, the same course and the same crowd.

From the sunrise start to the finish arch, every kilometre is marked, supported and cheered. Aid stations every 5K, pacers for every target time, and medals for everyone who crosses.

  • 20K+ runners
  • 4 distances
  • 8 aid stations

The route

A point-to-point loop from Central Plaza along the river, across the old bridge, and back to the finish arch.

  1. Start Balai Kota
  2. Checkpoint 1 Jalan Veteran
  3. Checkpoint 2 Pesawat Suhat
  4. Checkpoint 3 Lapangan Rampal
  5. Finish Balai Kota
